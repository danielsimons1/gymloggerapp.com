Privacy Policy

Last updated September 11, 2026

Gym Logger is a workout planning and logging app for iPhone, made by EOTT Tech ("we", "us"). This policy says what we collect, why, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. It's written to be read, not to cover us.

The short version. We collect what you tell us in setup and what you log in the gym, because the app can't write your plan without it. We use a language model to write the plan, so a summary of your profile is sent to that provider. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

What we collect

Your account

You sign in with Apple. We receive the name and email address Apple shares with us — which, if you choose "Hide My Email", is a relay address rather than your real one. We use it to identify your account and for nothing else. We don't send marketing email.

Your training profile

During setup you tell us your goal, experience level, the equipment you have, where you train, how many days a week, how long a session, and optionally your age, gender, height, weight, and any injuries or limitations. Everything optional is optional: the app works without height and weight, and gives effort-based guidance instead of suggested loads.

Some of this is health-related information. We collect it for one purpose — writing training that's safe and appropriate for you — and we don't use it for anything else.

Your training log

The workouts you do, the sets, reps and weights you log, when you trained and for how long, and any notes you write on a session. This is the core of the app and it's stored on your account so it follows you between devices.

Usage analytics

We use Amplitude to understand how the app is used — which screens are opened, where people drop out of setup, whether a feature is working. These events are tied to an anonymous identifier, not your name or email. We don't send your training log or your profile details to Amplitude.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user id and your purchase receipt from Apple; it never sees your payment details, and neither do we.

What we don't collect

Your location, your contacts, your photos, or anything from other apps. We don't use advertising identifiers and there are no ads.

How the plan is written

Gym Logger uses a language model, provided by Anthropic, to write your plan and each next workout. To do that we send it a summary of your training profile (the answers above, including any injuries you reported) and, for each new workout, a summary of what you lifted recently. We don't send your name, email, or account identifiers with these requests.

Anthropic processes this data to generate the response and, under their API terms, doesn't use it to train their models. See Anthropic's privacy policy for their handling.

Exercise videos

When you open an exercise's form video, the app searches YouTube for a tutorial and plays it inline. The search sends the exercise name to Google's YouTube Data API, and the player is YouTube's embedded player, which is subject to Google's privacy policy. We don't tell YouTube who you are.

Where your data lives

Your account, profile, plan and training log are stored on our server, hosted on MongoDB Atlas. Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. A copy of your current plan and log is also kept on your device so the app works offline.

Who we share with

Only the providers named above — Apple, Amplitude, RevenueCat, Anthropic, Google/YouTube and MongoDB — and only the data described for each. They process it to provide their service to us. We don't sell personal data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.

We'd disclose data if legally required to, and we'd tell you unless the law prevented it.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. Delete your account and it's gone — see below.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete Account, inside the app. This removes your account, profile, plans and entire training log from our server and from the device. It's immediate and it can't be undone. Analytics events already sent to Amplitude are anonymous and aren't tied back to the deleted account.

Your subscription is managed by Apple and isn't canceled by deleting your account — cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Your rights

Wherever you are, you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Deletion is self-service in the app; for the rest, contact us and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're in the EU, UK or California you have specific statutory rights to these things, and we honour them without the paperwork.

Children

Gym Logger isn't for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think we have, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top is the date of the current version.

Contact

Questions about privacy go to support@gymloggerapp.com.
