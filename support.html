Support

We read everything and reply to most things within a day.

Email support@gymloggerapp.com. If it's a bug, say what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead — and your iPhone model and iOS version if you know them.

Common questions

My plan is stuck on "generating"

The first week takes about a minute to write. If it's been longer, leave the screen — generation continues in the background — and come back. If it fails, the app shows a recovery card with a "Finish my plan" button that picks up where it stopped, without replacing anything you've already trained.

I want to start over with a new plan

Settings → Regenerate plan. This replaces your current plan; your training history is kept.

I got injured

Open your profile from Settings and add the injury under limitations. The next workout is written around it. If something hurts during a session, stop — the plan can wait.

The rest timer isn't showing on my Lock Screen

Check Settings → Gym Logger → Live Activities is on, and that Focus modes aren't hiding it. The Live Activity starts with your first logged set of a workout.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Gym Logger. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it. Refunds also go through Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete Account, in the app. It removes everything — account, profile, plans and training log — from our server and your device, immediately. See the privacy policy for what we hold and why.

Can I use it on iPad or Android?

iPhone only for now.
